Regional airline · Tax Advisory

Lifted automated invoice matching from ~25% to 83% so every rotable part could be valued

  • ProblemAfter the airline's recapitalisation, the tax team needed the cost of each rotable part bought, but the evidence sat in scanned PDFs.
  • ApproachTurned 50,971 PDFs into 72,990 invoices with Azure Document Intelligence, then matched them in Databricks with PySpark rules and a human review layer.
  • Result83% matched by count and value, enough to rebuild the FY22–FY26 depreciation schedule.

How I work

Every number ties out

Pipeline

Bronze, silver, gold

Raw files land untouched, get cleaned and typed, then shaped into the tables people actually use. Each layer is rerunnable on Databricks.

Reconciliation

Prove it before reporting it

Totals reconcile back to the source at every step. Exceptions go to a human review queue instead of being quietly dropped.

Communication

Answer first

Findings lead with the conclusion and the number behind it, then the method, written for a partner or lawyer with five minutes.
